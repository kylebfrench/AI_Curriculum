# AI Curriculum

## Purpose

This repository is the long-term home of my AI and computer science learning journey.

It exists to help me build a deep, connected understanding of modern AI systems, software engineering, and the technical foundations underneath them—not simply learn how to use individual AI tools.

This repository will serve as the durable source of truth for the journey: what I am learning, what I have practiced, what I have built, where my understanding is strong or weak, and how the different concepts connect over time.

## Guiding Principles

- Understand before implementing.
- Learn why before how.
- Build in very small, connected steps.
- Foundations come before abstractions.
- Use AI aggressively as a teacher and engineering assistant, but do not let AI hide what is happening underneath.
- Prefer genuine understanding over memorizing terminology.
- Use hands-on labs, projects, assessments, reflection, and explanation to reinforce learning.
- Treat confusion as a signal to slow down and break the concept into smaller pieces.
- Keep the learning system adaptable as AI technology changes.

## Long-Term Goal

Develop the ability to understand, design, build, debug, operate, and continuously learn modern AI-enabled software systems—including unfamiliar technologies I have never encountered before.
